<objective>
Give call groups and hold music the same adapter treatment as the migrated domains — these two are named write targets in the coverage decision and have no tools at all today.

Purpose: D-15 draws the write boundary to include call groups and hold music alongside routes and the seven pre-existing domains. Neither has ever been reachable by the agent, so unlike the migration plans this is new surface rather than replaced surface. They pair well: both are small, both are referenced by other domains, and both fail in the same way — a group pointing at a departed subscriber rings nobody, and a queue pointing at a removed hold-music class plays silence to a waiting caller.

Output: two adapters with read and proposal-gated write tools, reference validation, and two domain skills.
</objective>
