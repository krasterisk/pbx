<objective>
Migrate the six voice-menu and queue mutations onto adapters, and correct the classification that currently calls a routing change harmless.

Purpose: the handwritten update tools for voice menus and queues are marked non-destructive, which is how a change that silently redirects every inbound caller ends up executing without a confirmation. D-18 makes any change to call handling a proposal regardless of how the old flag classified it. These two domains are also where a wrong reference is worst: a menu digit pointing at a destination that does not exist drops the caller, and a queue whose overflow target is gone strands them on hold. Both are checkable before confirmation, so this plan checks them.

Output: two adapters covering six migrated tools plus their read tools, reference validation, and two domain skills.
</objective>
