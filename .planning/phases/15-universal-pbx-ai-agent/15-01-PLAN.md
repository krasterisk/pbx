<objective>
Make `McpToolsService.callTool()` the single hardened dispatch point and give the registry the contract surface the rest of Phase 15 builds on.

Purpose: D-22 must be a mechanical guarantee, not a convention. Today the registry contract forbids closing over `vpbxUserUid`, but nothing stops the model from putting `vpbxUserUid` inside tool arguments, and the destructive gate accepts `confirm: true` from the model itself — which is agreement by an obedient model, not by a human (D-18/D-19 replace it). This plan also adds `getDomains()`, required by the D-16/D-17 completeness gate in 15-23, and the `AgentDiffProposal` type consumed by 15-05 and every adapter plan.

Output: sanitized dispatch, bootstrap-time registry build, `AgentDiffProposal` contract, and a table-driven per-tool tenancy test that grows by itself as adapters land.
</objective>
