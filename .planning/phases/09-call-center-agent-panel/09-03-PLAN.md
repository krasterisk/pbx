| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-09-03-01 | Spoofing | agent-channel Dial* events | mitigate | findAgentByChannel only matches known logged-in agents; unmatched channels are ignored, so a stray channel cannot inject KPI/status for another tenant |
| T-09-03-02 | Information Disclosure | agentKpiUpdate SSE | mitigate | emitEvent is keyed by userUid and routed through the existing per-tenant SSE stream (CallCenterSseController); no cross-tenant fan-out |
| T-09-03-03 | Tampering | counter integrity | mitigate | Counters computed server-side only (never client-trusted); sinceMidnight restored from DB on reconnect to prevent drift |
</threat_model>
