## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-10-01-01 | Information Disclosure | getMyContacts cross-tenant leak | high | mitigate | Every query filters `where: { user_uid: req.user.vpbx_user_uid }`; tenant id from JWT only (copy getMissedCalls) |
| T-10-01-02 | Elevation of Privilege | operator edits/deletes another operator's contact via crafted uid | high | mitigate | Ownership folded into where clause (`created_by = userId` unless isSupervisor); UI hiding is not the boundary |
